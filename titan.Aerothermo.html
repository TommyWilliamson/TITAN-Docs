    Aerothermo#

    Aerothermo - aerothermodynamic analysis and ray tracing

    This module includes functionality to calculate the loads induced upon an assembly object by the aerothermodynamic environment.

    Impinging hypersonic flow pushes (the “aero” part) and heats an assembly (the “thermo” part).

    In order for us to compute the pressure, shear and heat flux on an object we need to deploy an aerothermodynamic model. In TITAN these models are split into high and low fidelity methods.

    For high fidelity aerothermo modelling refer to the CFD SU2 and DSMC SPARTA sections. These modules interface with high fidelity tools.

    Low fidelity aerothermo modelling is handled in the aerothermo module.

    Aerothermo module#

    aerothermo module - TITAN’s low fidelity aerothermodynamics models

    The low fidelity models applied here fall under the category of raytraced panel codes, raytracing is used to determine if a face (i.e. panel) of the mesh can be seen by the flow. For more information refer to the raytracing module.

    In the rarefied regime TITAN deploys the Schaaf-Chambre model that determines pressure and shear coefficients.

    In the continuum regime TITAN uses Modified Newtonian Theory (MNT) to determine the pressure coefficient (Cp) on a face In MNT Cp is solely a function of a maximal stagnation pressure and the squared sine of the angle of incidence of the face.
